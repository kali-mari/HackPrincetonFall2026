Read DESIGN.md, LAYOUT.md, and the impeccable context. Use the fullstack-engineer skill. Restyle the board built in F1 and F2. Do not change behavior, sizes, or tile positions.

- Color each tile by its wordType's Fitzgerald Key token.
- Tiles: 64px symbol above a 24px bold label in Atkinson Hyperlegible, rounded corners, a 2px darker border of the same hue.
- Press feedback: the tile darkens and scales to 0.97 for 120ms, so the child sees the press registered before audio plays.
- Sentence strip: a white bar with a strong border; each chosen word appears as a mini tile in its own color. Speak is the most prominent button on screen, green, with a speaker icon and the word "Speak".
- While audio plays, the sentence strip gets a gentle pulse on its border (none with prefers-reduced-motion).
- Load Atkinson Hyperlegible from Google Fonts with font-display: swap.

Done when: a screenshot reads as a calm, colorful AAC board, not a generic web app, and every word type is distinguishable by color.
