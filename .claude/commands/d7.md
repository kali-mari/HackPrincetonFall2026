---
description: D7: visual check of the real render in Chrome
---

Use the chrome-browser skill. With the dev server running, open http://localhost:5173 in a new Chrome tab.

1. Take a screenshot at 1280x800 and another at 1024x768.
2. Check against DESIGN.md and LAYOUT.md: nothing scrolls, no text is cut off or overlapping, tiles match LAYOUT.md sizes, every word type has its color, Speak is the most prominent thing on screen.
3. Press Space a few times and Enter once, screenshot each state, and check the scanning highlight is obvious on every tile color.
4. Read the console and list any errors or warnings.
5. List each problem with the screenshot it appears in, then fix them one at a time and re-screenshot to confirm.

Done when: a final pair of screenshots shows no listed problems and the console is clean.
