description: D5: animate the scanning highlight (after F3)
---

Use the impeccable skill's animate command on the switch scanning highlight in the AAC board. Read DESIGN.md and LAYOUT.md.

- Group level: the whole active group gets the violet 6px outline, drawn inside its boxes, plus a faint tint behind it. Every other group dims to 60% opacity.
- Item level: only the active item gets the outline plus a 1.05 scale; the rest of its group stays at full opacity, other groups stay dimmed.
- When the highlight has stepped past the last item and covers the whole group again (the back out state), make that state visually distinct, for example a dashed outline.
- Transitions of 150ms max. With prefers-reduced-motion, no scaling or fading; only the outline moves.
- Check that the highlight is visible on every Fitzgerald color, including yellow tiles.

Done when: standing 3 meters from the laptop, a teammate can always say what is highlighted.
